# 提示词编译

**视觉命题：** 平静、完整的外表内部压着一股无法维持秩序的力量。

**构图骨架：** 超近景切片。一个平静侧脸占画面 70%，一条斜向裁切让后景失序。

**色调：** 深钴蓝承重，暖象牙停顿，黑墨统一轮廓，朱砂红作为唯一事件色。

**细节岛：** 面部轮廓与眼睛是一个岛；衣领和少量文字边带是第二岛。

**材质断点：** 只有眼睛内部出现一小块镜面金属，其他部分保持统一印刷表面。

**negative constraints：** 禁止符号拼盘、多焦点、彩虹色板、光滑三维、贴纸叠放和均匀细节。

```text
Graphic-surreal vertical editorial poster, single calm profile filling 70 percent of the frame.
Compressed cobalt-blue field, warm ivory skin and near-black ink; one small vermilion event color.
One diagonal crop opens a second spatial layer without adding extra characters.
Two detail islands only: the facial structure and one restrained collar/text band.
A continuous bold-black contour, posterized shadows, halftone, screen-print misregistration and paper grain unify the full surface.
One dominant material break: chrome reflection confined inside the eye.
Readable silhouette at thumbnail size. Negative: symbol pile, multiple focal points, rainbow palette, glossy 3D, sticker collage, uniform detail, watermark.
```
